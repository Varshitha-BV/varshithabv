About This App

This Personal Expense Tracker is a lightweight, client-side app for tracking income and expenses. It stores data in your browser's Local Storage and provides a dashboard with charts and budget tracking.

Features

  • Add income and expense transactions with category, date and notes.
  • View your balance, total income and expenses.
  • Dashboard with charts for category breakdown and trends.
  • Set a monthly budget and track progress.
